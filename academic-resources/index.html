Prepare a Clear Research Account

  1. State the research question and intended contribution.
  2. Define the system, context, and assumptions.
  3. Explain methods and connect claims to evidence.
  4. Compare alternative interpretations and report limitations.
  5. Document data, code, and sources sufficiently for examination.
