Research Themes

  • Causal Explanation and Scientific Reasoning

    How do event sequences, mechanisms, and counterfactual accounts support explanation? Relevant work may compare competing explanations, identify assumptions, or examine how uncertainty changes a scientific conclusion.

  • Models and Virtual Experiments

    Mathematical models and simulations describe possible behavior. Research can investigate how their outputs become interpretable scientific accounts, how model assumptions are communicated, and how simulated outcomes are tested against observations.

  • Intelligent Systems and Human Understanding

    Explanations of autonomous behavior should connect system inputs, constraints, decisions, and outcomes. Topics include interpretable decision support, human–machine collaboration, knowledge representation, and the evaluation of explanations for different users.

  • Parallel Systems and Collective Behavior

    Interactions among physical processes, computational representations, and social actors create multiple accounts of a shared situation. Relevant questions concern coordination, feedback, conflicting objectives, and the traceability of decisions across agents.

Engineering Applications

  • Robotics and Autonomous Systems

    Explain robot actions through goals, environmental observations, planning constraints, and feedback. Evaluate whether these explanations help operators identify failures, understand limitations, or collaborate safely.

  • Manufacturing and Industrial Operations

    Connect sensor observations, process models, and maintenance records to explain equipment behavior. Potential studies include fault diagnosis, production scheduling, root-cause analysis, and knowledge transfer between operators.

  • Transportation and Infrastructure

    Study traffic scenarios, vehicle interactions, and infrastructure responses through linked sequences of events. Compare alternative interventions using simulation, field measurements, and clearly stated performance criteria.

  • Energy and Environmental Systems

    Relate forecasts and operational decisions to physical constraints, uncertainty, and environmental impacts. Applications include energy management, resilient networks, and the interpretation of sustainability trade-offs.

  • Agriculture and Biological Modeling

    Use growth models, environmental observations, and virtual experiments to explain development over time. Distinguish measured processes from model assumptions and evaluate how explanations support practical decisions.
